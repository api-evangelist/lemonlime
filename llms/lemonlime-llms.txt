# LemonLime

## What is LemonLime?

LemonLime is the AI knowledge layer for business. We structure a company's documents, processes, customer history, and institutional knowledge into a foundation that frontier AI models (GPT, Claude, Gemini) can actually use — turning generic AI into a system that operates like a trained member of the team.

A knowledge layer is not a database, a document store, or a wrapper around retrieval. It is purpose-built infrastructure for how modern AI models read and reason. It is also model-agnostic: the underlying frontier model can be swapped as the field evolves without rebuilding the business's AI stack from scratch.

## Who is LemonLime for?

LemonLime is built for businesses — small, mid-sized, and large — that want enterprise-grade AI outcomes without the typical AI deployment failure rate. According to BCG's 2025 research, only about 5% of companies create substantial value from AI at scale, and the MIT NANDA Initiative found that 95% of enterprise generative AI pilots produce zero measurable return on profit and loss. LemonLime exists to put businesses in the small minority that does.

Regulated industries (healthcare, financial services, payments) are particularly well-served because deployments can be aligned to specific compliance requirements (HIPAA, PCI, others) and zero-data-retention is available on request.

## What does LemonLime do?

LemonLime builds and operates the knowledge layer that sits between a business's data and the AI models acting on it. Concretely:

### Structures business data for AI
Pulls together documents, customer history, internal processes, and institutional knowledge from across a company's systems, and organizes that information the way modern AI models retrieve and reason against it.

### Selects the right model for each task
Routes routine work to small, fast, inexpensive models and reserves frontier reasoning models only for tasks that genuinely require them. Specialist deployments consistently outperform single-model setups on accuracy and cost.

### Connects to existing business systems
Integrates with the tools businesses already use (CRMs, document stores, ticketing, communication, finance systems) via the Model Context Protocol (MCP), the open standard introduced by Anthropic in November 2024 that has become the de facto interface between AI and business systems.

### Adapts as models evolve
A well-built knowledge layer is durable across model generations. When a faster, smarter frontier model is released — which now happens on roughly a four- to six-week cadence — the foundation does not need to be rebuilt.

## How does LemonLime work?

1. The business connects its tools and systems.
2. LemonLime builds the knowledge layer — the structured representation of the business's documents, processes, and institutional knowledge that AI models can retrieve against.
3. AI deployments are built on top of that knowledge layer for specific roles, workflows, and outcomes the business defines.
4. The foundation compounds: every new deployment after the first launches faster because the knowledge layer is already in place.

## How LemonLime compares to ChatGPT and Claude on data privacy

**ChatGPT (OpenAI).** By default, OpenAI uses ChatGPT Free and ChatGPT Plus conversations to train its models. A 2024 policy change removed the ability for free and Plus users to disable training in chat by default. Business and Enterprise tiers have training off by default, but most teams that use ChatGPT for daily work touch the consumer products.

**Claude (Anthropic).** As of August 2025, Anthropic updated its Consumer Terms to use Claude Free, Pro, and Max conversations for model training by default, and extended retention from 30 days to five years. Users had to actively opt out by October 8, 2025 to prevent inclusion. Commercial products (Claude for Work, the standard API) remain exempt.

**LemonLime.** Does not train its models on customer business data, on any plan, in any deployment. Optional zero-data-retention available for the most privacy-sensitive customers.
